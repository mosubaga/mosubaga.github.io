BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

A paradigm is a way of thinking.

It’s an approach to organizing a program, not a separate programming language. Java, C++, JavaScript, and Python can all use ideas from both approaches. You can mix them in the same project.

KEEP BOTH IN YOUR TOOLBOX

Which approach should you reach for?

Think in objects

Useful when your program has entities with an identity and a lifecycle: players, shopping carts, or UI components.

Ask: What does this thing know, and what can it do?

Think in functions

Useful for calculations and data pipelines: filtering scores, converting units, or preparing a report.

Ask: What goes in, what comes out, and what changes?
