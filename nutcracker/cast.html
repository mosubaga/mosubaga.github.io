The Leads

Main Characters

C
Heroine

Clara (Marie)

A curious, kind-hearted young girl who receives the nutcracker doll as a Christmas gift. Her bravery — throwing her slipper at the Mouse King — turns the tide of the whole story, and the rest of the ballet unfolds through her eyes.

N
Hero

The Nutcracker Prince

An enchanted wooden doll who leads the toy soldiers against the mice. Once freed from his curse, he's revealed to be a prince, and escorts Clara to the magical Land of Sweets.

D
Mentor & Magician

Herr Drosselmeyer

Clara's mysterious, charismatic godfather and a master toymaker. He gifts the nutcracker and seems to know far more about the night's magic than he lets on.

M
Antagonist

The Mouse King

The fearsome, multi-headed leader of the mouse army. He commands his troops against the toy soldiers until Clara's quick thinking helps the Nutcracker defeat him for good.

S
Royalty

The Sugar Plum Fairy

The graceful ruler of the Land of Sweets. She welcomes Clara and the Prince, and performs one of ballet's most iconic solos — set to the shimmering sound of the celesta.

F
Comic Relief

Fritz

Clara's rowdy little brother, who breaks the nutcracker doll during the party — setting a very eventful Christmas Eve into motion.
