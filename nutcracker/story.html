Act I, Scene 1

Christmas Eve at the Stahlbaum House

The Party

It's Christmas Eve, and the Stahlbaum family is hosting a grand party. Children dance around a towering, candle-lit tree while their mischievous little brother Fritz causes trouble among the toys.

A Curious Gift

Clara's godfather, the eccentric toymaker Herr Drosselmeyer, arrives with marvelous mechanical gifts — and for Clara, a carved wooden nutcracker doll. Fritz snatches it and breaks it, but Drosselmeyer repairs it, and Clara cradles it close for the rest of the night.

Midnight Strikes

Long after the guests leave, Clara sneaks downstairs to check on her nutcracker. As the clock strikes twelve, the room begins to change: the Christmas tree grows to the ceiling, and the toys wake up all around her.

Act I, Scene 2

The Battle of the Mice

✦

The Mouse King Attacks

An army of mice, led by the fearsome seven-headed Mouse King, swarms the room. The Nutcracker springs to life, rallies Clara's toy soldiers, and leads them into battle.

Clara Saves the Day

Just as the Mouse King corners the Nutcracker, Clara hurls her slipper at him, breaking his concentration long enough for the Nutcracker to defeat him.

The Transformation

Freed from the mouse curse, the nutcracker doll transforms into a handsome prince. Grateful, he invites Clara on a magical journey — first through a glittering forest of dancing snowflakes (the "Waltz of the Snowflakes"), and onward to his home.

Act II

The Land of Sweets

✦

A Royal Welcome

Clara and the Prince arrive in the Land of Sweets, ruled by the Sugar Plum Fairy. In her honor, the kingdom puts on a dazzling celebration of dances from around the world.

A Parade of Dances

Spanish chocolate, Arabian coffee, Chinese tea, and the high-flying Russian Trepak each take a turn, followed by the playful "Dance of the Reed Flutes" and Mother Ginger, whose enormous skirt hides a troupe of tiny clowns.

The Grand Finale

The Sugar Plum Fairy and her Cavalier perform a dazzling pas de deux, and the whole court joins the sweeping "Waltz of the Flowers." In many productions, Clara then wakes up beneath her own Christmas tree — leaving us to wonder whether it was ever a dream at all.
